Why DeepSight Strategy

Built for the Decisions That Shape What Comes Next.

I'm Jackie Parker. I've spent more than two decades leading talent, organizational, and business transformation, including work at Global Payments, General Motors, and Newell Rubbermaid, with earlier experience at Pepsi-Cola, Quaker Oats, and Nabisco. Today, I bring that perspective directly to founders and business owners navigating growth and the decisions that come with it.

What I've Learned

What Got You Here Won't Always Get You There.

Growth changes the demands on a business. Decisions become more complex. Leadership responsibilities shift. Systems that once worked begin to strain. And the owner can find themselves carrying more than they should.

I've seen these challenges inside large organizations. Sustainable growth takes more than adding people or working harder. It takes capable leaders, clear accountability, disciplined execution, and a willingness to change what no longer works. I help business owners see what needs attention, make informed decisions, and move forward.

"The right advice should give you clarity about the decision, and practical help moving it forward."

What I Believe

Growth requires a different kind of leadership.

What works at one stage may not sustain a business at the next.

Clarity creates capacity.

Clear ownership and decision-making allow good people to act.

Technology should solve business problems.

AI and automation matter when they improve the way work gets done, not simply because they are new.

Good advice should lead to action.

I ask difficult questions, give candid counsel, and help turn decisions into results.

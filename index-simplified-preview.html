When It Helps to Have Someone Outside the Company

As a company grows, some decisions become harder to work through with the people closest to them.

  • ✓ The leadership team may not be ready for what the business requires next.
  • ✓ A senior hiring or succession decision has significant consequences.
  • ✓ The culture or organization is not keeping pace with growth.
  • ✓ The strategy has changed, but the leadership and organization have not caught up.
  • ✓ The CEO wants a direct, experienced perspective without internal politics.

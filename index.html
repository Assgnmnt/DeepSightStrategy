Common Questions

What Founders Ask Before They Reach Out

How are you different from a business coach or a traditional management consultant?

We are operational builders, not deck-makers. A coach works on you and a traditional firm hands you a report. We build the roles, decision rights, and weekly rhythms alongside your team, then hand them off so your people run them. You work directly with Jackie Parker, with no junior team behind her.

Will you bury our 20-person company in corporate bureaucracy?

No. Jackie Parker has spent more than 20 years inside large companies, and we use that pattern recognition to keep yours lean. We install only the structure that takes a decision off your desk, and nothing that adds a layer.

What does Hand-Off Work actually include, and what do you expect our team to run?

We design and install three things: role clarity and decision rights, a lightweight 90-day priority and weekly scorecard rhythm, and repeatable hiring scorecards, 30-60-90 onboarding playbooks, and practical AI workflows. Then we hand them off for your internal leads to run. We do not act as full-time recruiters sourcing resumes, and we do not stay on as permanent staff running your weekly meetings.

How much time does an engagement require from me as the founder?

Your time goes to the decisions only you can make. We scope the time commitment with you before anything begins, and the aim is to take work off your desk, not add to it.

How does the Founder Scale Session™ work, and what happens after I submit the intake?

There are three steps. You complete a brief five-minute intake about your business. Jackie Parker reviews it personally, confirms fit, and invites you to schedule within two business days. Then we run a focused working session that produces your Hand-Off Map: what you keep, what you hand off, and what we take on.

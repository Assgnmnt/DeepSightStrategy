About Jackie

Jackie Parker

Experience that is useful in the room.

I have spent more than 20 years working inside large, complex companies, including Global Payments, General Motors, and Newell. Most of that time, I was the senior executive brought in to work through the leadership and organization decisions that mattered most.

I have advised senior leaders through hiring and succession decisions, culture and organization change, and the moments when a company's strategy moves faster than its leadership team. I have sat on the inside of those decisions, with the consequences that come with them, not just observed them from outside.

I founded DeepSight Strategy™ to bring that experience directly to founders and business owners working through similar decisions today. This is a principal-led practice. When you work with me, you work with me, not a team, not a framework, not a deck. I bring what I have learned to the company in front of me, and I tell you what I actually think.

I work with a small number of founders and business owners at a time, by design. Every conversation starts with understanding what you are working through, and whether I am the right person to help.

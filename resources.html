Research

Delegating: A Huge Management Challenge for Entrepreneurs

Gallup, April 2015

Among the fast-growing Inc. 500 leaders Gallup studied, those with high delegation talent averaged a three-year growth rate 112 points higher than those with limited or low talent. Gallup also found that three in four employer entrepreneurs have limited-to-low delegation talent.

My take. Gallup found strong delegation talent is uncommon among owners and linked it to faster growth. Worth knowing before you decide how much to hold onto.

Research

2024 Small Business Problems and Priorities

NFIB, 2024

Owners rated how critical 75 problems are to their business. "Managing employees" was called critical by 6.5% of owners with 1 to 4 employees and 16.1% of owners with 20 to 49 employees, where it ranks 9th of the 75.

My take. The people side of the job gets heavier as the team grows. That is the point where the owner is usually the one in the middle.

Research

2026 Report on Employer Firms: Findings from the 2025 Small Business Credit Survey

Federal Reserve Banks, March 2026

The survey reached more than 6,500 small employer firms across the United States. Owners' expectations for revenue and employment growth declined to their lowest levels since 2020, though still well above that low point.

My take. Useful for context before you plan next year. Read it for what owners across the country are bracing for.

Research

Q2 2026 Confidence Index and Burnout Survey

Vistage, July 2026

Vistage surveyed 1,351 US leaders of small and midsize businesses between June 1 and 15, 2026. Seventy percent said they experience burnout at least occasionally.

My take. A sample of Vistage members, so it speaks for leaders who already seek out peer support. Still worth knowing how common the load is.

Case example

I Couldn't Afford My First Employee, But Hiring Her Helped Me Grow a Million-Dollar Business

Emily Grey, The Flourish Market, via Buffer, November 2022

The owner of a boutique hired an experienced full-time store manager when she could cover only about three months of that salary. She reports revenue more than tripled within the year, and reached $1 million in revenue less than a year later. This is the owner's own account.

My take. A real example of taking something off the owner's plate before it felt affordable. One owner's story, not a rule.

How-to

How to Find the Right Candidates When Hiring for Your Business

Greg Smith, Entrepreneur, January 2025

Walks through calculating the full cost of a hire, deciding what you are ready to hand off, judging candidates on documented accomplishments, and writing down what success looks like at 30, 60 and 90 days.

My take. The 30, 60, 90 day piece is the part I would steal first. Hiring is half of delegating. Being clear about the job is the other half.

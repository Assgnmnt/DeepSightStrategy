How We Partner

Here Is What Happens
When We Work Together.

Your company keeps waiting on you. We work directly with you, and you work directly with Jackie Parker. We find what only you should decide, move everything else to someone who owns it, and build the structure that keeps it moved. No menu. No junior team.

What We Are Brought In To Fix

When the Company Runs Through You

You built something that works. Now it works because you are in it. Here is what that looks like from the inside.

Decisions wait for you

Work stops until you weigh in, so the speed of the company is the speed of your calendar.

Good people stay small

They bring you problems instead of solving them, because no one ever wrote down what they are allowed to decide.

You are the last review

Quality depends on you catching things, which means you can never really step back.

Leader hires do not stick

You hire a strong person, hand them a vague role, and end up taking the work back.

Engagement Models

Two Ways to Partner

We take on real work, make it visible, and build AI-ready structures to hand it off.

01

Hand-Off Work

What we do

Write down who decides what, define roles and accountability, set a weekly operating rhythm, and help you hire or onboard the leader who takes the work.

What you do

Make the calls only you can make, and introduce us to the people taking the work.

What exists after

Written hand-offs and AI-enabled workflows your team can run without you in the room.

What we install

Role clarity and decision rights, lightweight 90-day priority and weekly scorecard rhythm, hiring scorecards, 30-60-90 onboarding playbooks, and practical AI workflows where repetitive work slows your team down.

What we do not do

We do not act as full-time recruiters sourcing resumes, and we do not stay on as permanent staff running your weekly meetings.

02

Ongoing Partnership

What we do

We stay on as your advisor and thought partner, take on work that has no owner yet, and keep the weekly rhythm honest.

What you do

Bring us the decisions you should not make alone.

What exists after

A company that keeps moving while you are out, and someone senior to think with when it matters.

Every engagement starts with a Founder Scale Session™.

What You Walk Away With

Things You Can Hold Up

Every engagement leaves behind working documents, not a slide deck.

Hand-Off Map

One page showing what you keep, what you hand off, and what we take on.

Who-decides-what chart

The decisions your people can make alone, the ones that need a check-in, and the ones that stay with you.

Role and accountability sheets

For each person taking work: what they own, how it is measured, and who they answer to.

Weekly operating rhythm

The short meeting set that replaces the ad hoc check-ins that now route through you.

Hiring profile for the next leader

What the role actually requires, written before you interview anyone.

Written hand-offs

Each piece of work you moved, documented so it holds when people change.

The First 90 Days

What a Typical Start Looks Like

Timing shifts with the size of the company and how much we take on. This is the usual shape.

Weeks 1 to 2

Founder Scale Session. You leave with the Hand-Off Map and a clear first priority.

Weeks 3 to 6

We build the first hand-off, starting with the one costing you the most time or money.

Weeks 7 to 12

The new rhythm runs. We adjust what is not holding and move the next piece of work.

Day 90

You decide whether to continue as an ongoing partnership. Either answer is fine.

The Adoption Read

Where is adoption breaking down?

Three movements. Choose the answer closest to where you actually are, not where you'd like to be.

01

Model

Whether leadership is visibly living it

Adoption follows what leaders visibly do, not what they approve in a budget meeting.

How visibly does your leadership team use AI themselves, not just approve it for others?
If your board asked for a clear story connecting AI investment to results, could you give them one today?
02

Move

Whether pilots ever become real work

Most organizations don't have an AI problem. They have a workflow ownership problem wearing an AI costume.

What's the honest status of your AI pilots?
Who owns redesigning how work actually changes because of AI?
03

Prove

Whether anyone can show it's working

This is usually the honest answer, not a number. Most companies never built a way to track it.

Can you point to a specific, measurable result AI has produced this year?
